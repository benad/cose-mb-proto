COSE microblogging prototype

Single-recipient profile 2 with payload and text encoding version 20261115. Everything runs locally in this page; no server or network connection is needed.

Private keys are not encrypted. Anyone with a private keypair can sign as you and decrypt messages sent to you. Save it securely. Keys and plaintext are visible to your browser and any extensions with access to this page.

Nothing is stored by this application. Closing or clearing the page is not a guarantee of secure erasure from browser memory, history, extensions, or your clipboard. Keep private keys out of shared devices and public posts.

    
Supply externally authenticated public keys. This prototype does not manage trust, rotation, replay detection, group messages, or platform integration. Compromise of a recipient's static private key exposes past messages; this profile does not provide forward secrecy against that compromise.
